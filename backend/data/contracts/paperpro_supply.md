# Supply Agreement – Office Supplies

**Between** PaperPro GmbH, Offenbach ("Supplier")
**and** Müller Feinmechanik GmbH, Frankfurt am Main ("Customer")

Start date: 1 January 2024

## §1 Subject matter
The Supplier delivers a monthly office supplies package as specified in Annex 1.

## §2 Delivery
Deliveries take place in the first week of each month.

## §3 Fees
The monthly package costs EUR 1,200.00 (net), invoiced on the 5th of each month.

## §4 Price adjustments
**§4.1 Price indexation.** Prices are adjusted once per calendar year in line with the German consumer price index (VPI), by no more than 5% per year.

**§4.2 Notice.** Price adjustments under §4.1 must be announced in text form at least 30 days before they take effect.

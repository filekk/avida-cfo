# Rahmenliefervertrag – Stahl und Aluminium (Framework Supply Agreement)

**Between** Stahlhandel Nord GmbH, Hamburg ("Supplier")
**and** Müller Feinmechanik GmbH, Frankfurt am Main ("Customer")

Start date: 1 January 2025

## §1 Subject matter
The Supplier delivers rolled steel and aluminium bars as ordered by the Customer under this framework agreement.

## §5 Fees
The monthly material delivery costs EUR 14,000.00 (net), invoiced on the 20th of each month.

## §6 Price adjustments
**§6.1 Material price adjustment.** The Supplier may adjust the prices in line with the published steel and aluminium price index, by no more than 8% per calendar year.

**§6.2 Notice.** Price adjustments under §6.1 must be announced in text form at least six (6) weeks before they take effect.

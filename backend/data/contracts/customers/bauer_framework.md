# Framework Agreement – Precision Parts

**Between** Müller Feinmechanik GmbH, Frankfurt am Main ("Supplier")
**and** Bauer Maschinenbau AG, Darmstadt ("Customer")

Start date: 1 March 2024

## §1 Subject matter
The Supplier manufactures precision parts according to the Customer's monthly call-off orders.

## §7 Payment
**§7.1 Payment terms.** Invoices are payable within 30 days of the invoice date without deduction.

**§7.2 Changes to payment dates.** Agreed payment dates may only be changed by written agreement of both parties.

**§7.3 Late payment.** In case of late payment, statutory default interest under §288(2) BGB applies.

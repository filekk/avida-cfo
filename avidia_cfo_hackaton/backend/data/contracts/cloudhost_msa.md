# Master Services Agreement – Business ERP Hosting

**Between** CloudHost GmbH, Hamburg ("Provider")
**and** Müller Feinmechanik GmbH, Frankfurt am Main ("Customer")

Commencement date: 1 September 2025

## §1 Subject matter
The Provider hosts and operates the Customer's ERP system ("Business ERP Hosting") in a German data centre.

## §2 Service levels
The Provider guarantees an availability of 99.5% per calendar month.

## §3 Data protection
The parties conclude a separate data processing agreement pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR.

## §4 Support
Support is available on business days from 08:00 to 18:00 CET.

## §5 Fees
**§5.1 Fees.** The Customer pays a monthly fee of EUR 9,800.00 (net) for the Business ERP Hosting plan, invoiced on the 15th of each month.

**§5.2 Price guarantee.** The fees under §5.1 are fixed for a period of 24 months from the commencement date (1 September 2025). Price increases during this period are excluded.

**§5.3 Price adjustments after the fixed period.** After expiry of the price guarantee, the Provider may adjust the fees with at least three (3) months' written notice. The Customer may terminate the agreement with effect from the date of the adjustment.

## §9 Term and termination
**§9.1 Term.** The agreement runs for an initial term of 36 months and renews automatically for 12 months unless terminated with 3 months' notice.
